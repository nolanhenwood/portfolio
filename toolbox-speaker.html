Mini toolbox speaker

Giving old car audio parts a new life as a working toolbox speaker.

SolderingElectronicsHands-on fabricationRepurposing

Project overview

Over the summer, I built a working speaker using a mini toolbox, car speakers, and a radio I pulled from a junk car. I wired the salvaged radio into the system and used a power brick to run it from a wall outlet.

My work

I mounted the speaker to the top of the toolbox with tapping screws, soldered the wiring, and connected the system to a power brick. The finished build plays music through the salvaged car radio and speakers.

Solving the wiring challenge

At first, I wasn’t sure which wires needed to connect to the speakers and power connections. I looked at wiring diagrams to identify where the wires belonged, then soldered the connections and got the system working.

What I learned

The project strengthened my soldering and troubleshooting skills and taught me how to use wiring diagrams to make sense of unfamiliar components. It also showed me how discarded car parts could be repurposed into something useful.
