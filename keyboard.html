04 / Personal project · Product design

Keyboard & numpad

A custom keyboard and hardwired numpad combination designed around an everyday interface.

Product designCustom buildHands-on making

Project overview

I designed both a keyboard and a separate numpad as a hardwired combination. The project brought together an interest in making custom objects and the practical details of a tool used every day.

My role

I designed both boards, considering the keyboard and numpad as parts of one setup. This project was an opportunity to take an idea for a personal interface and turn it into a physical build.

Perspective

Working on a familiar object encouraged me to think about how design decisions shape the experience of using a product.
