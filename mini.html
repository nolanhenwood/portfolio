Mini Cooper restoration

Diagnosing problems, replacing parts, and learning how vehicle systems work together.

TroubleshootingMechanical repairHand tools

Project overview

Working on my Mini Cooper has been a hands-on way to learn about automotive systems. The project includes mechanical repairs, routine maintenance, and interior restoration.

What I worked on

  • Replaced components including the radiator, intake manifold, air intake, timing chain tensioner, oxygen sensor, coolant temperature sensor, and starter motor.
  • Replaced brake pads and rotors, and performed maintenance including oil changes, a brake bleed, and work with coolant and power steering fluid.
  • Worked on the window assembly and interior details including the seat, trim, speakers, and tachometer.

Problem solving

When troubleshooting a coolant leak, I checked multiple potential sources before replacing the radiator. That process reinforced the value of investigating a problem carefully before settling on a repair.
