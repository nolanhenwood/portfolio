01 / UW BARC Lab · Robotics

Tank cleaning robot

Mechanical design, prototyping, and hands-on support for an NDI tank cleaning robot.

SolidWorks3D printingAssembly

Project overview

At the Boeing Advanced Research Collaboration at UW, I contribute to a robot intended to clean a nondestructive inspection tank. The work has given me a chance to connect digital designs with the practical needs of a physical machine.

My contributions

  • Updated the CAD model to reflect the physical robot and support ongoing design work.
  • Designed 3D-printed board holders that clip into U-channels, helping secure components within the robot.
  • Made quick prototypes using readily available materials, including cardboard stands and paperclips for holding boards.
  • Worked on spacers and hole locations for metal plates, and helped with assembly, testing, troubleshooting, and documentation.

Design thinking

I also explored a side-opening access concept for waterproofing. The project has shown me how important it is to consider access, fit, and assembly alongside the initial design.
